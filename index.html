About Our Church

Cebu Christ Covenant Tabernacle is a family of believers committed to knowing Jesus Christ, growing in faith, and serving our community with love. Whether you've been a Christian your whole life or are just beginning to explore faith, there is a place for you here. We believe in the power of God's Word, the grace of the cross, and the transforming work of the Holy Spirit.
